Important information about SahodPH calculations, estimates, limitations, and sources of payroll information.

Informational estimates

SahodPH provides salary and workplace pay calculators for informational and planning purposes. The results are estimates generated from the information entered by the user and the calculation rules implemented for each tool.

A result shown by SahodPH should not be treated as an official payroll computation, payslip, tax assessment, employment determination, or government-issued calculation.

Why your actual payroll may be different

Actual compensation can depend on details that are not included in a particular calculator. Your employer may also use payroll policies, compensation structures, divisor conventions, benefits, exemptions, adjustments, or other deductions that affect the final amount you receive.

For this reason, the amount calculated by SahodPH may differ from the amount shown on your actual payslip.

Government contributions and taxes

Some SahodPH calculators estimate items such as SSS, PhilHealth, Pag-IBIG, and withholding tax based on the rules and assumptions implemented in the calculator.

Government contribution schedules, tax rules, payroll regulations, and other applicable requirements can change. Users should verify important or time-sensitive figures against the latest information published by the relevant government agency.

Employment and payroll decisions

SahodPH does not provide tax, legal, accounting, employment, or financial advice. Calculator results should not be used as the sole basis for accepting a job offer, making a financial decision, resolving a payroll dispute, or determining a legal entitlement.

If you need an official determination about your salary, contributions, taxes, benefits, or employment rights, consult your employer's payroll department, a qualified professional, or the appropriate government agency.

No government affiliation

SahodPH is an independently created informational website. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, sponsored by, or operated by SSS, PhilHealth, Pag-IBIG, BIR, or any other Philippine government agency.

Calculator limitations

The calculators are designed to provide practical estimates based on the information available to them. They may not account for every possible payroll situation, employment arrangement, benefit, deduction, exemption, or special compensation rule.

Users are responsible for reviewing the inputs they provide and checking whether the assumptions used by a calculator are appropriate for their particular situation.
