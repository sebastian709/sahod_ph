Reading the table

Five things the numbers show

1. No income tax until about ₱22,730 a month

The first ₱20,833.33 of monthly taxable compensation is tax-free, and taxable compensation is what's left after SSS, PhilHealth, and Pag-IBIG. At a ₱22,727 salary, contributions total ₱1,893.18, which leaves ₱20,833.82 taxable. That's the point where tax starts. Everyone below it pays only contributions, which is why the deduction rate actually falls from 9.5% at ₱10,000 to 8.4% at ₱22,000.

2. SSS stops growing at ₱34,750

The SSS Monthly Salary Credit is capped at ₱35,000, which is reached at a salary of ₱34,750. From there up, the employee share stays at ₱1,750 whether you earn ₱35,000 or ₱200,000.

3. PhilHealth keeps rising until ₱100,000

PhilHealth is 2.5% of basic salary with no cap until ₱100,000, where it reaches ₱2,500. Between ₱35,000 and ₱100,000, it's the only contribution that still grows with your pay.

4. Pag-IBIG is ₱200 for almost everyone

With the maximum fund salary at ₱10,000, every employee earning ₱10,000 or more pays exactly ₱200. You can contribute more voluntarily, but it isn't required.

5. Deductions rise gradually, not in jumps

Crossing into a higher tax bracket only affects the pesos above the threshold. That's why the "total deducted" column climbs smoothly, from 9.6% at ₱25,000 to 20.2% at ₱100,000. No raise can leave you with less take-home pay. See the salary comparison for how much of a raise you keep.

Assumptions behind the table

  • The salary is monthly basic pay of a regular private-sector employee.
  • The employee is not a minimum wage earner. Minimum wage earners pay no income tax on their minimum wage, but at those salary levels the table already shows ₱0 tax.
  • SSS, PhilHealth, and Pag-IBIG are the employee shares for one month. Your employer pays its own share on top of your salary.
  • Withholding tax uses the BIR monthly table in effect since 1 January 2023. Your employer's December year-end adjustment may raise or lower your final withholding slightly.
  • There are no allowances, overtime, loans, or absences. Add those in the salary calculator's Advanced mode.
