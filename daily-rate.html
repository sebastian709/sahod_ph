Daily salary guide

How to calculate your daily rate in the Philippines

A daily rate is an estimate of how much your monthly salary represents for each working day. It can be useful when you want to understand your daily earnings, compare compensation, or use a daily amount as part of another payroll calculation.

Daily rate formula

Estimated daily rate = Monthly salary ÷ Working days per month

For example, if your monthly salary is ₱42,000 and you use 26 working days as the divisor:

₱42,000 ÷ 26 = ₱1,615.38 per day

The calculator lets you change the number of working days so you can use a divisor that matches the assumptions used by your employment contract or payroll policy.

Why can daily rates be different?

There is not always one universal divisor that applies to every employee. Monthly-paid and daily-paid employees can be treated differently, and employers may use different payroll conventions depending on the employee's work schedule and compensation structure.

Because of this, the daily rate shown by this calculator should be treated as an estimate based on the monthly salary and working-day divisor you enter.

When can a daily rate be useful?

Knowing your estimated daily rate can help when:

  • Comparing monthly and daily compensation
  • Understanding how much your monthly salary represents per workday
  • Estimating certain workplace pay calculations
  • Reviewing salary information before accepting a job offer
  • Comparing different work schedules or compensation arrangements

Monthly salary vs. daily rate

Monthly salary and daily rate are not interchangeable. Your monthly salary represents the agreed monthly compensation, while a daily rate expresses that amount using a selected number of working days.

For example, the same ₱42,000 monthly salary produces a different estimated daily amount when divided by 22 working days instead of 26 days. This is why choosing the appropriate divisor is important.

Monthly Salary Working Days Estimated Daily Rate
₱42,000 22 ₱1,909.09
₱42,000 24 ₱1,750.00
₱42,000 26 ₱1,615.38

Does the daily rate determine your actual payroll?

Not necessarily. Your employer's payroll system may use a specific divisor or calculation method for a particular purpose. Overtime, holiday pay, leave, absences, and other payroll calculations can also have their own applicable rules.

If you are checking an actual payroll calculation, compare the result with your employment contract, company payroll policy, and official payslip.

Common questions

Daily rate calculator FAQs

How do I calculate my daily rate from my monthly salary?

Divide your monthly salary by the number of working days used as the divisor. The appropriate divisor can depend on your employment arrangement and payroll policy.

What divisor should I use?

Use the working-day divisor that applies to your employment arrangement or the purpose of your calculation. If you are unsure, check your employment contract or ask your employer's payroll department.

Is daily rate the same as take-home pay?

No. Daily rate is a way of expressing monthly compensation as a daily amount. Take-home pay is the amount remaining after applicable deductions.

Can I use the daily rate for overtime or holiday pay?

A daily rate can be relevant to some payroll calculations, but overtime and holiday pay can have their own applicable rules. Use the appropriate SahodPH calculator for those calculations.

Why does my company's daily rate differ from this calculator?

Your employer may use a different divisor or payroll convention. The calculator is an estimate based on the monthly salary and working-day count that you enter.
