Overtime pay guide

How overtime pay is calculated

Overtime pay is additional compensation for qualifying work performed beyond the employee's regular working hours. The amount can depend on the employee's hourly rate, the number of overtime hours, and the applicable premium for the day or situation.

Overtime pay formula

Estimated overtime pay = Hourly rate × overtime hours × applicable multiplier

For example, if your hourly rate is ₱250 and you worked 2 overtime hours using the 125% option:

₱250 × 2 × 1.25 = ₱625

The result represents the estimated overtime compensation for the hours entered. It is separate from the employee's regular pay for the normal working hours.

Why your overtime pay may be different

The calculator provides a simplified estimate based on the hourly rate, overtime hours, and multiplier you select. Actual payroll can involve additional factors that are not represented by this tool.

Differences can result from factors such as:

  • The employee's regular work schedule
  • The classification of the day worked
  • Holiday or rest-day status
  • Applicable company payroll policies
  • Other compensation or payroll adjustments

Overtime pay is different from your regular hourly pay

Your regular hourly rate represents your normal compensation for each regular working hour. Overtime pay applies an additional premium to qualifying hours worked beyond the regular schedule.

For this reason, you should not assume that every additional hour is automatically paid at the same rate as a regular working hour.

What this calculator does not determine

This calculator does not determine whether particular work qualifies as overtime or which legal rate applies to a specific employment situation. It estimates the amount based on the inputs and multiplier selected by the user.

If you are checking an actual payslip or overtime dispute, compare the result with your employment contract, company payroll policy, and the applicable official labor guidance.

Common questions

Overtime pay calculator FAQs

How do I calculate overtime pay?

The calculator multiplies your hourly rate by the number of overtime hours and the overtime multiplier selected for the calculation.

Is overtime pay the same as my regular hourly rate?

No. Qualifying overtime hours can be subject to an additional premium. The applicable multiplier depends on the situation being calculated.

Does overtime pay change when I work on a rest day?

The applicable calculation can change depending on the day and work situation. Select the option that matches the situation you are trying to estimate.

Can I use my hourly rate from the SahodPH calculator?

Yes. You can use an estimated hourly rate as the starting point for an overtime calculation, provided the rate matches the assumptions of your calculation.

Why is my employer's overtime calculation different?

Actual payroll can depend on your work schedule, holiday or rest-day status, compensation structure, payroll policies, and applicable rules. SahodPH provides an estimate rather than an official payroll determination.
