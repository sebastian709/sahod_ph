Understand your estimate

How SahodPH estimates take-home pay

This Philippine salary calculator starts with the monthly income you enter, estimates standard employee contributions and withholding tax, then shows the estimated take-home pay. Use Advanced mode when you need to include allowances, overtime, night differential, or bonuses.

Gross salary

Gross salary is your total monthly income before deductions. It can include basic salary, taxable allowances, non-taxable allowances, overtime, night differential, and bonuses or incentives.

SSS contribution

The calculator estimates your employee share of the Social Security System contribution using the applicable salary credit limits and contribution schedule.

PhilHealth contribution

PhilHealth is estimated from the applicable premium rate and income floor and ceiling, with the employee share shown as a monthly deduction.

Pag-IBIG contribution

The Pag-IBIG amount represents the estimated employee contribution based on the applicable compensation tier and contribution cap.

Taxable income

Taxable income includes the earnings entered as taxable and excludes allowances marked non-taxable. Mandatory employee contributions are then considered before estimating withholding tax.

Withholding tax

Withholding tax is an estimate based on the applicable Philippine income-tax brackets for monthly compensation. Your employer’s payroll calculation may use additional details.

Estimated take-home pay

Estimated take-home pay is your gross monthly income less government contributions and estimated withholding tax.

What is a salary calculator in the Philippines?

A Philippine salary calculator helps you estimate the difference between gross pay and net pay. SahodPH brings together the earnings you enter, estimated SSS, PhilHealth, Pag-IBIG, and withholding tax so you can better understand your monthly salary computation before payroll is processed.

Example salary computation

For a quick estimate, enter your basic monthly salary, then add any applicable taxable or non-taxable allowances, overtime, night differential, or bonuses in Advanced mode. The breakdown separates gross income, government contributions, estimated withholding tax, total deductions, and estimated net pay.

Philippine salary calculator FAQs

Does this calculate take-home pay in the Philippines?

Yes. The calculator estimates monthly take-home pay by subtracting estimated government contributions and withholding tax from your gross monthly income.

Which salary deductions are included?

The estimate shows employee SSS, PhilHealth, Pag-IBIG, and withholding tax, along with total deductions.

Is this the same as my employer's payroll?

No. This is an informational estimate. Actual payroll can vary with benefits, exemptions, payroll policies, and applicable government rules.
