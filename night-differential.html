Night shift pay guide

How night differential is calculated

Night differential is an additional amount paid for qualifying work performed during designated night hours. SahodPH lets you enter your hourly rate, the number of qualifying night hours, and the applicable premium rate to estimate the additional amount.

Night differential formula

Estimated night differential = Hourly rate × qualifying night hours × differential rate

For example, if your hourly rate is ₱201.92, you work 8 qualifying night hours, and the selected differential rate is 10%:

₱201.92 × 8 × 10% = ₱161.54

The result represents the estimated additional night differential, rather than your total pay for the hours worked.

What does the 10% rate mean?

The calculator uses a 10% night differential rate as its standard estimate. This means the additional amount is calculated as 10% of the hourly rate for the qualifying hours entered.

The applicable rate and qualifying hours can depend on the employee's situation and the rules that apply to the work being performed. Always verify important payroll figures against the applicable employment and labor rules.

What is included in the calculation?

The calculator uses three inputs:

  • Your hourly rate
  • The number of qualifying night hours
  • The night differential rate

These values are multiplied together to estimate the additional night differential for the hours entered.

Night differential is not the same as your total night-shift pay

The night differential is an additional premium. It does not represent the entire amount you earned for working those hours.

For example, with an hourly rate of ₱201.92 and 8 qualifying hours, the regular pay for those hours would be based on the hourly rate, while the night differential represents the additional premium calculated by this tool.

Why your actual night differential may be different

Actual payroll can depend on your employment arrangement, qualifying work hours, compensation structure, applicable premium rates, employer payroll policies, and other circumstances that are not represented by this simplified calculator.

If you are checking an actual payslip, use your employer's payroll records and the applicable official labor guidance as the final reference.

What this calculator does not determine

This tool does not determine whether a particular hour qualifies for night differential or whether a particular employee is legally entitled to the premium. It provides an estimate based on the inputs you enter.

Common questions

Night differential calculator FAQs

How is night differential calculated?

This calculator multiplies the hourly rate by the number of qualifying night hours and the selected differential rate.

What rate does this calculator use?

The default rate in the calculator is 10%. You can change the rate in the input field when your calculation requires a different assumption.

Is night differential my total salary for the night shift?

No. The result represents the estimated additional night differential. Your regular pay for the hours worked is separate.

Can I use my hourly rate from the SahodPH calculator?

Yes. An estimated hourly rate can be used as an input, provided that the rate and assumptions match the calculation you are trying to make.

Why is my employer's calculation different?

Payroll calculations can depend on the employee's work schedule, compensation structure, qualifying hours, applicable rules, and employer payroll practices.
