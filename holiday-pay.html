Holiday pay guide

How Philippine holiday pay is estimated

Holiday pay can depend on the type of holiday and whether the holiday falls on an employee's rest day. SahodPH lets you enter your daily rate and select the holiday situation so you can estimate the corresponding pay amount.

Regular holiday

When work is performed on a regular holiday, the calculator uses the regular-holiday multiplier selected in the tool. The estimated amount is based on the daily rate you enter.

Estimated holiday pay = Daily rate × applicable multiplier

Special non-working day

A special non-working day is treated differently from a regular holiday. The calculator provides a separate option for this situation so the applicable estimate can be viewed independently.

When a holiday falls on a rest day

Your rest-day status can affect the applicable holiday pay calculation. If a holiday also falls on your scheduled rest day, select the corresponding rest-day option in the calculator.

Example holiday pay calculation

Suppose your daily rate is ₱1,615.38 and you work on a regular holiday. Using the 200% option in this calculator, the estimated amount is:

₱1,615.38 × 2.00 = ₱3,230.76

If a different holiday or rest-day situation applies, select the corresponding option to see how the estimated amount changes.

Why your actual holiday pay may be different

The calculator is a simplified estimate based on the daily rate and holiday option you select. Actual payroll can depend on additional employment and compensation details, including the applicable holiday classification, work schedule, rest-day status, company payroll policies, and other applicable rules.

If you are checking an actual payslip or resolving a payroll question, compare the calculation with your employment contract, company payroll policy, and the applicable official labor guidance.

What this calculator does not determine

This tool does not determine whether a particular date is officially classified as a regular holiday or special non-working day. It also does not replace an employer's payroll calculation.

Disclaimer: This calculator provides an estimate for informational purposes only. Actual holiday pay may vary depending on your employment situation, holiday classification, rest-day schedule, payroll policies, and applicable government rules.

Common questions

Holiday pay calculator FAQs

How is holiday pay calculated?

This calculator estimates holiday pay by multiplying the daily rate you enter by the multiplier selected for the holiday situation.

What is the difference between a regular holiday and a special non-working day?

They are different holiday classifications and can have different pay treatment. Select the classification that matches the situation you are calculating.

What happens when a holiday falls on my rest day?

A rest-day situation can result in a different applicable multiplier. The calculator provides separate options for the holiday and rest-day combinations supported by the tool.

Can I use my daily rate from the SahodPH Daily Rate Calculator?

Yes. You can use an estimated daily rate as the starting amount for this calculator. Make sure the daily rate and holiday classification match the assumptions used by your payroll.

Is this the exact amount my employer should pay me?

Not necessarily. SahodPH provides an estimate. Actual holiday pay can depend on your employment arrangement, holiday classification, work schedule, payroll policies, and applicable rules.
