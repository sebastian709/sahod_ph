Privacy Policy

A plain-language summary of how SahodPH handles information, analytics, advertising, and calculator inputs.

Last updated: September 24, 2026

About this policy

SahodPH is an independent informational website that provides Philippine salary and workplace pay calculators. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be processed when you use the website and how third-party services used by SahodPH may handle information.

Calculator inputs

Salary values and other information entered into the calculators are processed in your browser by JavaScript. SahodPH does not require an account and does not send calculator inputs to a SahodPH server as part of the normal calculator operation.

Because calculator inputs may contain information about your compensation, you should avoid entering information that you do not want processed in your browser or potentially exposed through your own device or browser environment.

Google Analytics

SahodPH uses Google Analytics to understand how visitors use the website. Analytics may collect information such as pages viewed, approximate usage information, browser and device information, and other measurement data.

Google Analytics uses cookies or similar technologies to provide analytics and measurement services. Information collected through Google Analytics is handled according to Google's applicable policies.

Google AdSense and advertising

SahodPH uses Google AdSense to display advertising. Google and its advertising partners may use cookies or similar technologies to provide, measure, and personalize advertising, subject to the settings and policies applicable to those services.

Advertising-related information may include information about visits to this website and interactions with advertisements. Third-party advertising providers may use this information according to their own privacy policies and applicable settings.

Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to SahodPH and/or other sites on the Internet.

You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings. You can also opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalized advertising at www.aboutads.info. To learn more, see how Google uses cookies in advertising.

Calculator inputs, such as the salary amounts you enter, are processed in your browser and are not sent to SahodPH or to advertising partners.

Cookies

SahodPH and third-party services used on the website may use cookies or similar technologies. Cookies can be used for purposes such as analytics, advertising, functionality, and measuring website usage.

You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings. Disabling certain cookies may affect how some websites or third-party services function.

Information you provide through email

If you contact SahodPH by email, the information you choose to include in your message may be received and processed for the purpose of responding to your inquiry, investigating website issues, or addressing feedback.

Please do not send passwords, financial account credentials, government identification numbers, or other sensitive personal information when contacting SahodPH.

External links

SahodPH may link to official government websites, professional resources, and other third-party websites for additional information. External websites operate independently and may have their own privacy policies, cookies, and data practices.

SahodPH does not control the privacy practices or content of external websites.

Data security

SahodPH takes reasonable steps to operate the website securely. However, no website or internet transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Changes to this Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the website's features, services, or data practices change. The date shown at the beginning of this page indicates when the policy was most recently updated.

Questions

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how SahodPH handles information, please contact us through the Contact page.
