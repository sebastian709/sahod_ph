13th month pay guide

How 13th month pay is calculated

13th month pay is generally based on the employee's total basic salary earned during the applicable calendar year. The SahodPH calculator provides an estimate based on the monthly basic salary and number of months worked that you enter.

13th month pay formula

Estimated 13th month pay = Total basic salary earned during the year ÷ 12

For a simple full-year example, suppose an employee earns ₱42,000 in basic salary each month for 12 months:

(₱42,000 × 12) ÷ 12 = ₱42,000

If the employee worked for only part of the year, the total basic salary earned during the applicable period is used as the starting point for the estimate.

What is basic salary?

Basic salary refers to the regular compensation paid for work performed. The treatment of other compensation items can depend on applicable rules and how those payments are classified.

Not every amount received by an employee should automatically be treated as basic salary for purposes of a 13th month pay calculation. Items such as allowances, overtime pay, and other benefits may have different treatment depending on the applicable rules and employment arrangement.

What if I did not work for the entire year?

Employees who worked for only part of the year can have a lower 13th month pay amount because the calculation is based on the basic salary actually earned during the applicable period.

The SahodPH calculator allows you to enter the number of months worked so you can estimate a partial-year amount.

Example for a partial year

For example, if an employee earns ₱42,000 per month and the simplified estimate assumes 6 months of basic salary:

(₱42,000 × 6) ÷ 12 = ₱21,000

This example assumes the monthly amount entered represents basic salary earned during each of those six months.

Why your actual 13th month pay may be different

Actual 13th month pay can depend on the employee's compensation history, salary changes during the year, unpaid periods, and the treatment of different compensation items.

The simplified calculator uses the information entered by the user. If your salary changed during the year, a calculation based on one constant monthly amount may not represent your actual payroll calculation.

Salary changes during the year

If your basic salary changed during the year, your total basic salary earned should reflect the amounts actually earned during the relevant months. For example, an employee who received a salary increase during the year may need to calculate the basic salary from each period separately.

The SahodPH calculator is intended as a quick estimate. For a more precise calculation involving multiple salary rates, calculate the applicable basic salary earned for each period before applying the formula.

What this calculator does not determine

This calculator does not determine whether a particular compensation item qualifies as basic salary under every employment situation. It also does not provide an official determination of an employee's entitlement or final payroll amount.
