How 13th month pay is calculated

Estimated 13th month pay is calculated by adding your basic salary earned during the calendar year, then dividing that total by 12. Regular allowances, overtime, and other benefits are generally excluded from the statutory calculation unless treated as basic salary under your employment policy.
