24 September 2026

Accuracy review of all calculators

Corrections

  • Overtime pay: overtime on rest days, special days, and regular holidays was computed at the premium rate for the day (130% or 200%) without the extra 30% overtime premium. It now uses the DOLE rates: 169% on rest days and special days, 260% on regular holidays, and 195%, 338%, 390%, and 507% for the combined cases.
  • SSS: the minimum Monthly Salary Credit was updated from ₱4,000 to ₱5,000 to match the 2025 SSS schedule. Only salaries below ₱5,250 are affected.
  • Night differential: the differential now applies to the hourly rate for the day, so rest days and holidays give a higher amount. The rate field now takes a percentage (10) instead of a decimal (0.10).
  • Daily rate: the calculator now uses the DOLE annual factor method (261, 313, or 365 days) instead of dividing by a number of days per month.

Added

  • Deduction tables: SSS, PhilHealth, Pag-IBIG, and BIR withholding tax tables, with official sources.
  • Take-home pay table for 22 salary levels from ₱10,000 to ₱200,000.
  • Holiday pay options for unworked regular holidays, double holidays, and special working days.
  • A night-shift option for overtime, and the 13th month tax-exemption check.
  • Worked examples, legal references, and a "last reviewed" date on every calculator page.

Changed

  • Site-wide navigation to every calculator and guide.
  • Removed empty advertisement placeholder boxes.
  • The privacy policy now explains Google's advertising cookies and how to opt out.

How SahodPH is kept up to date

The rates behind every calculator are rechecked against SSS, PhilHealth, Pag-IBIG Fund, BIR, and DOLE issuances when a new circular or wage rule is announced. Each change is logged here with its date. If you find an error, please report it.
