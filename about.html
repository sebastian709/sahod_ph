About SahodPH

A practical Philippine salary and payroll calculator designed to make common workplace pay questions easier to understand.

Why SahodPH was created

Salary information can be confusing when you are trying to compare job offers, check your expected take-home pay, or understand why the amount on your payslip is different from your advertised salary. SahodPH was created to make these common salary questions easier to calculate and understand in one place.

Instead of manually working through different salary deductions and payroll formulas, you can enter your compensation details into the appropriate calculator and review an estimated result. The goal is to provide a simple starting point before you make a personal financial or employment decision.

Who can use SahodPH?

SahodPH is intended for employees, job applicants, freelancers, career changers, and anyone who wants to better understand common Philippine salary and workplace pay calculations.

For example, you might use the salary calculator when comparing two job offers, estimate your monthly take-home pay, or check how government contributions and withholding tax can affect your gross salary.

What you can calculate

SahodPH currently provides several calculators covering common Philippine compensation questions:

  • Monthly salary and estimated take-home pay
  • Salary and take-home pay comparison
  • 13th month pay
  • Overtime pay
  • Holiday pay
  • Night differential
  • Daily salary rate
  • Hourly salary rate

Each calculator is designed for a specific type of calculation so you can review the assumptions and information relevant to that calculation instead of relying on a single generic salary estimate.

How SahodPH approaches calculations

The calculators use the information entered by the visitor together with the calculation rules implemented for each tool. Where a calculation involves government contributions or tax rules, the result should be treated as an estimate rather than a replacement for an official payroll computation.

Compensation can differ between employees. An employer may also have additional benefits, deductions, payroll adjustments, exemptions, or company policies that are not represented by a particular calculator.

Independent informational resource

SahodPH is an independent website. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by SSS, PhilHealth, Pag-IBIG, BIR, or any other Philippine government agency.

Government contribution schedules, tax rules, employment regulations, and payroll practices can change. For this reason, important employment and financial decisions should be confirmed using the latest information from the relevant government agency or your employer's official payroll department.

Using SahodPH responsibly

The calculators are provided for informational and planning purposes. A result from SahodPH should not be treated as an official payslip, tax assessment, employment contract, or legal determination.

If your actual payslip differs from an estimate shown by SahodPH, your employer's official payroll records should be used as the reference for your actual compensation and deductions.
