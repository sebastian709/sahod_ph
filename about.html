About SahodPH

A simple, practical place to estimate Philippine salary and workplace pay figures.

Making salary information easier to use

SahodPH is an independent informational website for employees, applicants, and anyone planning a career or compensation decision. Our calculators turn common salary questions into clear estimates that are easier to review and compare.

What you can calculate

The site includes a Philippine salary calculator, take-home pay estimates, salary comparison, 13th month pay, overtime, daily and hourly rates, night differential, and holiday pay tools. Each calculator explains its formula and highlights assumptions that can affect the result.

Independent information

SahodPH is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by SSS, PhilHealth, Pag-IBIG, BIR, or any Philippine government agency. Government rules and employer payroll policies can change, so use the results as a starting point and confirm important figures with your employer or the relevant official source.
